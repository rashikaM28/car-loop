Why this matters
Previously PCPs wrote referrals into a black hole — no visibility into whether patients ever booked or showed up. CareLoop pushes real-time status back into the PCP's EHR workflow via a Task FHIR resource update, closing the loop for the referring physician without any extra work on their part. This also creates a clinical audit trail for every referral.
